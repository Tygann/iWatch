iWatch: Movies & Shows — version 1.0 (39)

PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
iWatch helps movie and TV viewers organize a Watchlist, record watched movies and episodes, and follow show progress. It provides catalog details and regional viewing-availability links. It does not stream or download full movies or TV episodes. It is free and noncommercial, with no advertisements, paid features, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. It has no public posting, commenting, or messaging interface.

SETUP AND REVIEW
No iWatch account or sign-in is required for local tracking and discovery. Internet access is needed for searches and new catalog information. No sample files or special external hardware are needed.
1. Launch iWatch and open Search. Find a movie or show and open its details.
2. Use the viewing-status control to add it to the Watchlist. Open Movies or Shows to see the saved title.
3. Mark a movie watched to update its viewing history. For a show, open a season and episode and mark the episode watched to update progress.
4. Explore title details, cast/crew, trailers, and where-to-watch information. Trailers and provider destinations open external services.
5. Optional release reminders are in Settings > Notifications. Provider credits, privacy, and support links are in Settings > About.

iCloud sync uses the device's Apple Account when available. Settings > Your Library shows iCloud status and library counts. There is no separate iWatch iCloud login.

OPTIONAL TRAKT SYNC
Trakt is not required for local tracking or discovery. No Trakt demo account is supplied. Reviewers who wish to test optional sync can open Settings > Trakt Sync > Connect and authorize an existing Trakt account through Trakt's own sign-in page.
If Choose How to Start appears, Upload Pending Changes sends local pending changes before downloading Trakt. Use Trakt Library replaces the iWatch library after confirmation and discards local contents/pending uploads; use it only with disposable review data. The sync control is available when appropriate. Settings > Trakt Sync > Disconnect Trakt stops future sync on this device and retains the iWatch library.

iWatch does not create an iWatch account. Trakt authorization is handled by Trakt. Settings > Storage & Data > Erase All App Data removes the iWatch library/preferences and sends library deletion through iCloud, then disconnects Trakt on this device. It does not delete the separate Trakt account/history. Erasure is destructive and not needed for normal review.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
TMDB API/image CDN: title, episode, person metadata and artwork.
Trakt API/OAuth: optional Watchlist and watched-history sync.
Apple iCloud/CloudKit: private library replication between devices.
JustWatch via TMDB: regional provider availability and links.
YouTube: external trailer links.
tylerkeegan.com: support/privacy pages and Trakt OAuth return URL.

REGIONS
Core library/tracking behavior is consistent. Provider availability, release information, certifications, and catalog coverage may differ by region. The device locale supplies the country/region; no GPS access is needed. External services/content have their own availability.
