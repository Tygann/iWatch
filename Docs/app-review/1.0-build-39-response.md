```text
Hello App Review,

Thank you. Below is the requested information for iWatch: Movies & Shows, version 1.0 (39).

1. Physical-device demonstration
Video: [VIDEO URL]
Recorded on an iPhone 15 Pro running iOS 27.0.1 on November 2, 2026, using TestFlight version 1.0 (39). The recording begins at the Home Screen and shows app launch, movie/show browsing and tracking, Watchlist changes, episode progress, cast details, regional availability, iCloud library status, and the optional Trakt sync status screen. About at the end displays version 1.0 (39) and provider credits. It does not demonstrate a fresh Trakt login or disconnection.

2. Purpose and audience
iWatch is a personal movie and television tracking app for people who want to organize what they plan to watch and keep track of what they have watched. Users can search titles, maintain a Watchlist, record watched movies and episodes, and continue shows from their recorded progress. It provides catalog information and regional viewing-availability links. It does not stream or download full movies or television episodes.

3. Setup and review access
No iWatch account or Trakt login is required for local tracking. Internet access is needed to search and retrieve new catalog information. No sample files or special hardware are required.

Launch the app, open Search, search for a movie or show, and open its details. Use the viewing-status control to add it to the Watchlist or mark it watched. Open Movies or Shows to inspect the library. For shows, open a season and episode to record watched progress.

Optional Trakt sync: open Settings > Trakt Sync and tap Connect. Trakt is optional and no Trakt demo account is supplied. All local tracking and discovery features can be reviewed without it. To test optional sync, authorize an existing Trakt account on Trakt's own sign-in page and return to iWatch. If Choose How to Start appears, Upload Pending Changes sends pending local changes before downloading Trakt. Use Trakt Library replaces the iWatch library after confirmation; choose it only with disposable review data, as it discards local library contents and pending uploads. Review sync status and use Sync Now when available. To disconnect, use Settings > Trakt Sync > Disconnect Trakt and confirm. Disconnection stops future Trakt sync and retains the iWatch library; it does not delete the Trakt account.

iCloud library sync uses the device's Apple Account when iCloud is available. There is no separate iWatch login for iCloud. Local tracking remains available without Trakt.

iWatch is free and has no paid features, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or advertisements. It has no public posting, commenting, or messaging interface.
iWatch does not create an iWatch account. Optional Trakt authorization is handled by Trakt. Settings > Storage & Data > Erase All App Data removes the iWatch library and preferences and sends library deletion through iCloud; it disconnects Trakt on this device but does not delete the separate Trakt account or history. This is destructive and is not necessary to review normal tracking.

4. External services
- TMDB API and image CDN: movie, television, episode, and person metadata and artwork.
- Trakt API and OAuth: optional authorization and synchronization of the user's Watchlist and watched history.
- Apple iCloud/CloudKit: private replication of the user's library between their devices.
- JustWatch availability data supplied through TMDB: regional provider information and external availability links.
- YouTube: external trailer links; iWatch does not host the videos.
- tylerkeegan.com: support/privacy pages and the Trakt OAuth return URL.

5. Regional behavior
The core library and tracking functionality is the same across regions. Viewing-provider availability, certifications, release information, and available catalog data can differ by region and provider coverage. The app uses the device locale's country/region for regional availability; it does not require GPS location. External services and linked content are subject to their own availability.

6. Third-party content and authorization
Catalog metadata and artwork are obtained through the TMDB API. iWatch is a free, noncommercial project using TMDB under its published noncommercial developer API terms, subject to the applicable terms and attribution requirements.
TMDB's published API and attribution guidance: https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/faq

Trakt's API Use Policy permits apps that help users manage their own watch history and lists, subject to its requirements, without individual written approval for permitted uses: https://developer.trakt.tv/docs/api-use-policy

TMDB and Trakt credits appear in Settings > About, and viewing-availability information credits JustWatch. iWatch does not claim ownership of third-party artwork or provide full-length movie/TV streaming or downloads.

Privacy policy: https://tylerkeegan.com/iwatch/privacy/
Support: https://tylerkeegan.com/iwatch/support/

Please let us know if any additional information is needed to complete the review.
```
